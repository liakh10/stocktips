Deploy Stock Tips

Three contracts on Robinhood Chain, in order. The wallet that deploys the factory becomes the guardian: it can pause new coins, cancel a pending binding and propose an oracle after a 48 hour notice. It can never touch a jar, its NVDA or its fees

  1. Deploy the jar implementation, every jar is a clone of it
  2. Deploy the burner, it receives 15% of every harvest
  3. Deploy the factory with the oracle address that matches STIPS_ORACLE_KEY in Vercel
  4. Put the factory and the burner into config.js and push
  5. After $STIPS launches on Pons, set it on the burner, once and for good

No constructor, no owner. It is only a template

3 · Factory
