Reading the chain
How it works

Anyone can fill a jar. Only its handle can empty it.

Three calls, all of them open to anyone except the last one.

1

Open a coin for a handle

The factory launches it on Pons with a fixed 3% creator tax and sets the handle's jar as the fee recipient in the same transaction. There is no call that redirects it afterwards.

2

Harvest the fees

Anyone sweeps the curve and claims the Pons escrow. The jar keeps 15% for the $STIPS burn and buys NVDA with the rest, at no worse than the Chainlink price minus 2%.

3

The handle claims

It posts a code, the oracle signs it, and a 48 hour wait starts that the guardian or the current wallet can cancel. After that every harvest pays out to that wallet.
