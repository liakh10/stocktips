How it works

01

Open

Anyone opens a coin for a handle. The jar is created with it and receives every creator fee, 3% of each trade plus most of the Pons curve fee

02

Harvest

Anyone can sweep and harvest: 15% goes to the $STIPS burn, the rest buys NVDA at no worse than the Chainlink price minus 2%

03

Claim

The handle posts a code, the oracle signs it, and after 48 hours the jar pays out to that wallet, now and on every later harvest

Opening a coin for someone is not an endorsement by them. Until the handle claims its jar, nobody can move what is inside
